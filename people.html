Anuva Aishwarya

(she/her)

Presidential Young Professor and Principal Investigator

Anuva will join the Department of Physics at National University of Singapore in February 2027. Prior to that, she was a Postdoctoral Fellow in the group of Amir Yacoby at Harvard University. She obtained her doctorate from University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign in 2023 from the group of Vidya Madhavan.

When she is not working, Anuva enjoys photography, reading, gardening and traveling.
