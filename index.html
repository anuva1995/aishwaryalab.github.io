Structural dynamics · sensing · control

We study how structures move, fail, and can be made to behave.

We build instrumented systems and control strategies for structures under real-world loading — from vibration in bridges to fault detection in rotating machinery. Our work sits between mechanics, signal processing, and control theory.

What we work on

Three threads run through the lab

Each grounded in instrumented experiments rather than simulation alone.

01

Structural health monitoring

Sensor networks and inference methods that detect damage in civil and aerospace structures before it becomes visible.

02

Nonlinear vibration control

Active and semi-active control strategies for suppressing unwanted vibration in flexible structures and machinery.

03

Rotating machinery diagnostics

Signal-processing methods for early fault detection in bearings, gears, and turbomachinery under variable operating conditions.
